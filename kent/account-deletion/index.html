Delete your Frank by Kent account

This page lets users of the Frank by Kent app (Google Play package com.kentplc.intranet), published by Kent Engineers & Constructors Inc, request that their account and associated data be deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Enter the email address you registered with in the form below and select Request deletion. You can also reach this page from the app: open your profile in Frank by Kent and choose Delete profile.
  2. We send a verification email to that address. Open it and select the link to confirm that you want your account deleted.
  3. Your account is deleted, usually straight away and always within 2 hours of confirmation. You lose access to Frank by Kent at that point.

Questions

Can I cancel a deletion request?

Until you select the link in the verification email, nothing is deleted, so you can ignore that email to cancel. Once you confirm, deletion cannot be undone. You can create a new account at any time.

I did not receive the verification email

Check your spam folder. If it still has not arrived, contact [CONFIRM: support email] and include the email address you registered with.
